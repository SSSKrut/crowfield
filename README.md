# Crowfield

Crowfield is a team shooter where two sides fight over three flags. Capture the flags, run up the score, and try to find whoever said the bridge was clear.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/378918e3-4b11-4b71-98d8-73729bfb0c7c

Play **Valley** or **Desert**, an oil town between pumpjack fields and tank farms, solo with bots or bring friends online. Open the prebuilt `Crowfield.app` on macOS.
