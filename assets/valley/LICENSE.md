# Valley texture sources

The 1K diffuse maps below come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license), which releases its asset files under CC0 1.0. Crowfield uses the terrain maps as grayscale detail and tints the wall and wood maps in the renderer.

- [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock)
- [Dirt Aerial 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt_aerial_02)
- [Rocky Terrain 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02)
- [Painted Plaster Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall)
- [Wood Planks Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_dirt)
